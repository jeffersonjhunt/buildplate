One Off Stax: name and art direction

Every screen on this canvas is a pixel-exact render for screen type 7: 640×480, two bytes a pixel, RGB444 direct colour, double-buffered. The layout is a 40×30 grid of 16-pixel cells. The agent should match them pixel for pixel. The game is themed as a shift on a fabrication line: lines cleared are layers, levels are jobs, the score is output.

Title plates from the first draft, half size; the final title screen is on its own board. The stamp reuses the boot splash's VINTAGE FAST treatment.

Each block: an 8×8 design shown doubled, the same for the falling piece (a sprite) and locked blocks: light edge, upper and lower face, dark edge, a white glint. Ghost: every other pixel of the edge, base colour.
MID-80S RULES
  • One 5×7 face, monoline with cut corners after the OOE wordmark, drawn 2× in 16×16 cells (5× for the title). All caps.
  • Thin rounded 1-pixel frames in $443 with the label in orange, cut into the top edge.
  • Backdrops and shading use 4×4 ordered dither, which keeps the 80s grain and hides 12-bit banding. Dimmed to 75% behind play.
  • Shop-floor words: SHIFT, OUTPUT, JOB, LAYERS, LINE HOLD (pause), LINE STOP (game over).
  • The red rubber stamp is the one flourish: the tagline on the title, NEW HI SHIFT on game over.
