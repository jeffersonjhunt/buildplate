Line clear

  • Cleared rows flash solid white for 6 frames, then the stack above drops. Logic never waits for the flash; the next piece is already in play.
  • Callout words, centred in the box: SINGLE, DOUBLE, TRIPLE, QUAD on line 1 in $FA0; T-SPIN on line 1 with the count on line 2; B2B or COMBO n on line 2 in $C50.
  • This frame shows another job's backdrop: each job has its own, shown whole on the job card first. The change happens behind the card, between the two screens (spec, "Backdrops").
  • A QUAD or T-SPIN triggers the matching ES5503 effect, the loudest one the game has.
