Settings

  • Panel on cells (5,3)–(34,26). Rows every 34 px from y 92; labels at x 144, values at x 352.
  • Selected row: a $311 bar behind it, label $EDD, value $FA0, amber > at x 120.
  • Volume is a 10-step bar of 12×14 rounded blocks, $FA0 for on and $443 for off. It sets PB_VOLUME.
  • BACKDROPS: ON or OFF (plain $111, for anyone who finds them busy).
  • KEYS opens a remap list: press a key for each action; stored as HID usages in SETTINGS.DAT.
  • ESC saves and goes back.
