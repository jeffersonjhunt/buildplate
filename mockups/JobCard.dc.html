Job card

  • Shown at the start of a game and at every new job, about 2 s (120 frames), so each job's backdrop is seen whole before the panels cover most of it.
  • Band y 340–431 in $111 with $C50 rules top and bottom. JOB label $998; the number at 5× in $FA0; the song title at 3× in $EDD; the backdrop's name and the job's speed in $998; the small OOE mark at right.
  • The new song starts under the card. Gravity is paused; any key skips it.
  • Load the next backdrop from the card while the old job's last line clears, then wipe it in behind the band.
  • In overtime there's no new card; the job number just keeps counting in the SHIFT panel.
